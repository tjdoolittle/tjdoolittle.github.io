Common questions

Automatic detection didn't log a drive.
Open Settings in the app and check the setup list. On iPhone, location must be set to Always and Motion & Fitness allowed. Keep Background App Refresh on, and don't swipe Mileage Log away in the app switcher: iOS won't wake an app you've force-quit until you open it again. On Android, allow location All the time and set battery use to Unrestricted.
Some drives are missing that I didn't take for work.
Drives shorter than half a mile, or that never went faster than about 15 mph, are skipped so walks and parking-lot moves don't fill your list.
How is distance measured?
By adding up the distance between GPS readings during the drive. Inaccurate readings and GPS drift while parked are ignored. If the signal drops (a tunnel or parking deck), the gap is bridged in a straight line and noted on the trip.
Which mileage rate does it use?
The IRS standard business rate in effect on the day of each trip. For 2026 that's 72.5 cents per mile through June 30 and 76 cents from July 1. Tap the rate on the green sign to add or change a rate.
How do I get my log to my accountant?
Settings, then Export CSV. The file opens in Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets and lists every business trip with its rate and deduction, plus totals.
I'm getting a new phone.
Settings, then Save backup, and keep the file in iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or Files. On the new phone, install Mileage Log and tap Restore. Backups also move between iPhone, Android, and the web version.
