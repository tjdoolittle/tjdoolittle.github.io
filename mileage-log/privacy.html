Mileage Log

Privacy policy

Last updated October 30, 2026. Applies to Mileage Log for iPhone, Android, and the web.

The short version: Mileage Log doesn't collect any data. Your trips, locations, and settings stay on your device. There's no account, no server, no ads, and no analytics.

What the app stores, and where

Mileage Log saves your trips (date, miles, start and end points, purpose, client, notes), your saved place names, and your settings. All of it is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. It isn't sent to us or to anyone else.

Location

The app uses your device's location to measure the distance of your drives and to note where each trip started and ended. If you turn on automatic drive detection, it also uses location in the background to notice when a drive starts. Location is processed on your device. The app keeps only each trip's total distance and its start and end points, not your full route.

To show a street address for a trip, the app asks your phone's built-in map service (Apple Maps on iPhone, Google on Android) to look up the start and end points. That lookup is handled by your phone's operating system under Apple's or Google's privacy policy.

Motion and activity

With automatic drive detection on, the app uses your phone's motion and activity data (Motion & Fitness on iPhone, Physical activity on Android) only to tell when you're driving. It isn't stored or shared.

Notifications

Notifications are created on your device to tell you a drive is ready to review. No push notification service is used.

Exports and backups

When you export a CSV or save a backup, the app creates the file and hands it to you. Where it goes from there (Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, email) is your choice and is governed by that service.

Data we collect

None. We don't operate a server for this app and receive no information about you or your trips. Because we never receive your data, we can't see it, sell it, or share it.

Deleting your data

Delete trips in the app, or uninstall it to remove everything. Any backup files you saved yourself are yours to delete.

Children

Mileage Log is a business tool and isn't directed at children.

Changes and contact
